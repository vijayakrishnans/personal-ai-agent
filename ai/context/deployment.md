agent's own keys are the owner's, stored per environment and handed to the
Worker as Worker secrets by each deploy; the agent reads no wiring. What it
remembers, and the models added in its page, live in its Durable Object. What the workspace holds right now (every secret
by name, and its integrations and their health) is on the console's Secrets
and Integrations pages for `org_b4c022cbde054db3bb0e011a7befb2f5`.
